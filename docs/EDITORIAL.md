# Editorial workflow

The launch library contains eight original **unreviewed manuscripts**. Suggested author assignments come from the supplied clinical focus areas; they do not assert that those clinicians wrote, reviewed or endorsed the generated text. The named founder must verify and accept attribution before publication.

For each manuscript:

1. Confirm the intended reader, species, geography and clinical scope.
2. Read every linked source and verify that it supports the surrounding text. Replace broad source landing pages with the most specific available guideline/article; add year and DOI only when verified.
3. Check diagnostic uncertainty, emergency advice, contraindications and any local legal requirements. Do not add patient-specific treatment or context-free dosage instructions.
4. Confirm authorship, image permission, conflicts of interest and permission for imported content.
5. Save and submit the revision. Another founder takes the review, documents changes and approves only after completing the assessment.
6. Publish the approved revision. New edits withdraw the old publication and require a new review.

A reviewer cannot approve their own manuscript or take review when they are the assigned article author. Access records are managed outside the CMS. Review is substantive clinical work; clicking a button is not a substitute.

## LinkedIn imports

Try the public URL first. If direct access is blocked, an editor may explicitly permit the external reader or paste the content. Check completeness: metadata or login-wall text is not a full article. Preserve the source URL, repair formatting, add citations and review for the website's audience. Never represent a successful extraction as proof of permission or evidence quality.

## Corrections

Read the CMS inbox regularly. Withdraw disputed clinical content while reviewing a potentially harmful error. Record the reason in editorial notes, update sources, obtain independent approval and republish. Export manuscripts before major changes. Firestore transactions detect stale edits but do not provide a complete historical revision archive.

## Profiles

Use genuine professional portraits only with permission. Initials are the default until real photographs are supplied. Confirm degree information, professional roles, affiliations and links with each clinician. Do not fabricate registration numbers, affiliations, peer-review dates or testimonials.
